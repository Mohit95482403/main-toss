App Rules & Toss Guidelines

1. Toss Market Definition

All wagers placed in the Toss Market apply solely to the official coin toss performed by match referees and team captains prior to the scheduled match start time.

2. Market Locking Time

Wagering locks exactly 5 minutes before the scheduled coin toss or as soon as captain toss footage is broadcast. Any wager submitted after toss commencement is deemed void.

3. Match Abandonment & Weather

If the match is abandoned prior to the toss taking place, all stakes are refunded in full (Status: No Result). If the toss is completed and declared, subsequent rain or match cancellation does not affect toss settlement.

4. Settlement Speed

Official toss settlements are credited directly to your Available Balance within 120 seconds of official broadcast confirmation.
