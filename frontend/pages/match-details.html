1. Official Referee Coin Declaration

Settlement is based exclusively on the referee's official coin call result announced during pre-match TV broadcast.

2. Pre-Match Lock Policy

The toss market locks exactly 5 minutes before scheduled toss time. Any wager submitted after toss commencement is deemed void.

If the match is abandoned prior to the toss taking place, all stakes are refunded in full. If the toss is completed, subsequent rain does not void toss settlements.
